Milestones

Key moments in the history of the Singapore Aircargo Agents Association — from our founding in 1971 to our role in Singapore's air cargo industry today.

Timeline

SAAA Milestones

A decade-by-decade record of SAAA's growth, advocacy, training development, and industry partnerships.

1970s
1970s — Founding Era
  • 1971

    Formation of the Singapore Aircargo Agents Association

  • 1977

    Change of name to “The Singapore Aircargo Agents Association”

  • 1978

    Official adoption of the SAAA logo

  • 1979

    Standard Trading Conditions were implemented

1980s
1980s — Regional Expansion
  • 1982

    First Dangerous Goods Course was conducted

  • 1985

    SAAA together with Air Cargo Association of Korea (ACAK) mooted the idea of forming a Federation of Asia-Pacific Aircargo Agents Associations (FAPAA). FAPAA was formed in the same year

  • 1986

    First FAPAA meeting was held in Singapore

1990s
1990s — Advocacy & Community
  • 1991

    SAAA was invited to serve on the National Computer Board (NCB) Committee

  • 1993

    SAAA set up a Task Force to research and advise on Goods & Services Tax (GST)

  • 1995

    First SAAA Charity Golf Tournament was held in aid of the Dyslexia Association of Singapore

  • 1996

    SAAA celebrated its 25th Anniversary

  • 1997

    Chairman SAAA invited to participate in the 4th Brunei-Singapore Exchange delegation led by the Minister for Foreign Affairs & Minister for Law

2000s
2000s — Training & Security
  • 2000

    Forklift Driving Certification Course granted SDF

  • 2001

    SAAA Members invited to the International Civil Aviation Organisation (ICAO) Regional Dangerous Goods Seminar for Asia and Pacific region

  • 2002

    Hosting of the 12th AFFA General Meeting by SAAA & SLA

  • 2003

    Launch of CargoTec Pte Ltd at the Laguna National Golf and Country Club

  • 2007

    SAAA participated in the Career Fair Exhibition 2007

  • 2008

    SAAA, Airport Police Division and CAAS jointly organised the Regulated Cargo Agent Regime (RCAR) Outreach Programme

2010s
2010s — Recognition & Excellence
  • 2011

    SAAA participated in the first Aviation Open House under the invitation of CAAS

  • 2012

    SAAA was invited by CAAS to sit in the Air Cargo Manpower Advisory Committee

  • 2013

    Rebranding of SAAA@Singapore

  • 2014

    SAAA@Singapore awarded bizSAFE Level 3 certification from Workplace Safety and Health Council

  • 2015

    SAAA@Singapore awarded bizSAFE Partner certification from Workplace Safety and Health Council

  • 2016

    SAAA@Singapore recognised as having been part of the momentous milestone in Singapore’s journey as a Nation — SG50

  • 2016

    SAAA Cargo Services Pte. Ltd. awarded as 2016 Asia Pacific Top Performing IATA Accredited Training School (ATS), as well as a 2016 Premier Circle Member

For the full story of SAAA’s founding and early leadership, visit Our History.
