Our Programmes

SAAA offers a range of industry-focused programmes designed to develop talent, enhance skills, and advance the air cargo profession in Singapore.

Project IMDD

Project IMDD is a talent development initiative led by SAAA@Singapore as part of SAAA's industry programmes. It focuses on enhancing the logistics skills of young talents in Singapore's air cargo and logistics sector through mentorship, hands-on training, and long-term career development.

Partnering with ITE and the five polytechnics, the programme pairs students with forward-looking SAAA member companies through internships and work-study initiatives. By investing in skills competency training before company-specific development, Project IMDD aims to attract, develop, and retain the next generation of high-skilled, tech-savvy logisticians.

The initiative leverages digitalisation, sustainability, and generative artificial intelligence to address evolving supply chain challenges — building a sustainable talent pipeline for the air cargo industry.

  • ✓ Mentorship with partner logistics companies
  • ✓ SAAA skills competency training programmes
  • ✓ Partnerships with ITE and polytechnics
  • ✓ Career progression and further education pathways

Who It's For

For Employers: SAAA member companies can partner with Project IMDD to attract and retain young talent, access quality skills-based training, and build a loyal workforce that grows into skilled logistics professionals. Learn more for employers

For Applicants: Students and young talents exploring a future in logistics can gain hands-on experience across diverse roles, with holistic career progression beyond traditional internships. Learn more for applicants

Browse partner companies currently offering internship and work-study opportunities through Project IMDD.

How to Get Involved

Interested employers and applicants can explore the full Project IMDD hub for programme details, training schedules, company listings, and application information.

Programme Pathway

  1. 1

    Selection

    Interns are paired with pre-validated SAAA member logistics companies for internship or work-study placements.

  2. 2

    Skills Training

    Selected candidates undergo complimentary SAAA skills competency training throughout the programme.

  3. 3

    Mentorship

    Accelerated talent development through mentorship as full-time employees with partner companies.

  4. 4

    Further Education

    Direct entry to Work Study Degree or Work Study Specialist Diploma programmes.

Career Conversion Programme (CCP) for Air Transport Sector

The Career Conversion Programme (CCP) is a workforce initiative by the SkillsFuture Workforce Development Agency (SWDA) that supports mid-career individuals in transitioning into the air freight forwarding industry.

As the appointed Programme Partner, SAAA works closely with SWDA and participating employers to facilitate the implementation of the programme. Through structured classroom training and on-the-job learning, participants acquire industry-relevant knowledge, practical workplace experience and recognised certifications that prepare them for a rewarding career in the air transport sector.

The programme also supports employers in developing a skilled workforce while providing job seekers with opportunities to build long-term careers in the industry.

  • ✓ On-the-job training with participating companies
  • ✓ Structured classroom training
  • ✓ Industry-recognized certifications
  • ✓ Career progression opportunities

How to Apply

Interested job seekers may browse CCP-supportable job vacancies through the MyCareersFuture (MCF) portal and submit applications directly to participating employers.

Explore available opportunities here: CCP-supportable job vacancies on MyCareersFuture

Successful applicants will undergo the employer's recruitment process before commencing the Career Conversion Programme.

Application Process

  1. 1

    Access MCF Portal

    Job seekers can access the MyCareersFuture (MCF) portal via the CCP webpage.

  2. 2

    Browse CCP-supportable jobs

    Search and browse CCP-supportable job vacancies with participating employers on MCF.

  3. 3

    Submit application directly

    Submit your job application directly to the employer through MCF.

  4. 4

    Invited for interview

    Shortlisted candidates will be contacted by the employer to attend an interview.

  5. 5

    Offered employment

    Successful candidates will be offered employment by the participating employer.

  6. 6

    Commence CCP training

    Begin structured training and on-the-job learning under the Career Conversion Programme.
